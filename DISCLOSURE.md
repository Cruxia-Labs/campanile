# disclosure protocol

The instrument measures structure. When a live repository shows the xz shape — a class-level custody inversion while overall custody never inverts, in a class listed in SIGNATURE_CLASSES_V0 — that observation is handled by this table, published in advance so the procedure is a commitment rather than a reaction. Coordinator-first: an established coordinating body receives the observation before anyone else, because a structural measurement about a living project deserves review by people equipped to weigh it.

**Row zero, absolute: any signature observation goes to founder review FIRST. No external contact of any kind — coordinator, maintainer, or public — precedes that review.**

| observed | evidence package | default route | clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| The xz shape at a living repository's current HEAD, reproducible at the pin | a REDACTED custody vector — role labels in place of author strings, fence fields intact — plus method shas, the sealed preregistration reference, and a signed receipt; the unredacted vector stays sealed unless the coordinator requests it under their process | CERT/CC VINCE (https://www.kb.cert.org/vince/) | the coordinator's own published process (https://certcc.github.io/CERT-Guide-to-CVD/) — cited, never invented; the repository's maintainers are notified promptly after coordinator acknowledgement, never surprised by publication |
| The xz shape in an archived or unmaintained repository | the same redacted package | CERT/CC VINCE (https://www.kb.cert.org/vince/) — archived repositories cannot receive repository-level advisories (https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories), so the coordinator route holds | no active-maintainer clock exists; publication only after coordinator acknowledgement and founder decision |
| A signature that does not reproduce at the current pin | recorded internally; no external contact | none — re-measured at the next epoch | none |

Every row reports a MEASUREMENT. What an inversion is and is not travels in-schema with every event object; the vocabulary above is structural by construction and checked by the fence scan.
